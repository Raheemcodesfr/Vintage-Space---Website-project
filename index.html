It startedwith a rail.

  1. 2024

    BM — a Tanzanian photographer and videographer — started collecting thrifted vintage and passing it to friends. He put it on Instagram and called it Vintage Space, not a shop name, because he wanted a community, not a store.

  2. then

    The page became a meeting point for young creatives across Tanzania, swapping clothes and ideas online.

  3. now

    One pop-up invite and a big turnout later, the community moved offline. BM is now Creative Director, and the room keeps getting bigger.

What happensin the room

Gatherings across Dar es Salaam where art, fashion and music share one floor — built with local brands, artists, designers, musicians and businesses. Come as you are; that's the whole point.

  • Young designers bring their own labels and show them to a crowd that actually came to look.
  • Painters hang their work on the walls for the night.
  • Outfits get made for the night — fabric picked at the market and sewn by the young designers themselves.
  • Music in the same room. Workshops are on the way.
